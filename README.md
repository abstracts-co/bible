# a/bible

How did these texts get to us? What's actually in that cave, and what are we guessing from a broken pot?

Starting with the Dead Sea Scrolls. There's a timeline, a map, a couple of jars you can turn around, and papers worth reading slowly. I'd like to add translation comparisons and notes on individual manuscripts as we go.

You don't need Hebrew to ask a question. If you're making a claim, give us enough of the source to check it. A page number helps a lot more than a screenshot of a headline.

I'm Alex, [AGZ-TX](https://github.com/AGZ-TX). I'm starting this collection; I'm not claiming to be its resident biblical scholar. If a note gets something wrong, let's fix the note and keep the correction.

The Wall is for questions, pictures, reading notes and discussion. Artifacts are the things we're keeping together. Use Contribute to improve one. The sources and edit history are all here in this repo, and the community lives at [abstracts.co/a/bible](https://abstracts.co/a/bible).

## Rules

1. Bring the source with the claim. Author, edition and a page/figure/verse locator, please. An excavation report and an opinion about that report are different things.
2. Questions can be short. Research answers need evidence. If you haven't checked something, ask it as a question instead of writing it up as a fact.
3. We're working on texts and history here. People of any faith or none are welcome; converting each other and scoring points off somebody's religion aren't the project.
4. Quote the reading you disagree with fairly. No dogpiling, insults or appeals to your follower count.
5. Corrections stay attached to the work. Don't quietly replace a disputed date and make the old discussion impossible to follow.
6. Use photos, scans and translations we can legally share. Link an image we can't republish. Your agent's confident answer isn't a citation; you're responsible for checking it.

GitHub's and Apple's content rules apply too.

## What's here

New here? [Start here](artifacts/start-here/) is a four-stop route through the rest.

- [A discovery still unfolding](artifacts/timeline/): sixteen milestones from the first Cave 1 finds to the 2025 dating paper. The last entry is our own checkpoint, not a discovery.
- [Caves and landscape](artifacts/qumran-map/): a cave catalogue and a map with two approximate site pins. Caves 1–11 have no pins on purpose; nobody here has verified their entrance coordinates yet.
- [A Qumran scroll jar](artifacts/jar/): a three.js model of KhQ 1474 built from its catalogue measurements. The shape between those measurements is a guess, and its sources say which parts.
- [Anatomy of a scroll jar](artifacts/jar-anatomy/): a cutaway of jar, lid, linen and scroll, drawn from published size ranges rather than one object.
- [Shrine of the Book](artifacts/shrine-photo/): three Commons photographs, credited under each one.
- [Two papers, one scroll, one jar](artifacts/source-desk/): short source notes, with page numbers, on the 2021 handwriting paper, the 2025 dating paper, the Great Isaiah Scroll and jar KhQ 1474.
- [Mark 1:1–3, WEB and OEB](artifacts/translation-desk/): two free English translations side by side, quoted exactly. No winner picked.
- [Document indexes](indexes/README.md) for the two papers. Read the leaf, then cite the page.
