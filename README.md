# a/bible

Recent discoveries like the Dead Sea Scrolls, archaeology, and research still in progress. Maps, timelines and 3D finds.

This repository **is** the a/bible community on [Abstracts](https://abstracts.co/a/bible). Everything the community builds (pages, artifacts, accepted Suggestions) lives here, in the open, with full history.

- **Posts and replies** happen on Abstracts.
- **Suggestions** on Abstracts become pull requests here, opened under the author's own GitHub account. When moderators merge one, it shows as Approved.
- **Artifacts** (timelines, maps, 3D models, documents, custom pages) live in `artifacts/`. Every fact in them links to its source in `SOURCES.md` or next to the artifact.

## Rules

1. Cite sources for archaeological claims.
2. Respect every tradition; debate ideas, not people.
3. Share media you have the right to share.
4. GitHub's and Apple's content rules apply.

## Use of this content

Nobody may use this community's content to train AI models or for AI research, commercial or otherwise. See https://abstracts.co/robots.txt. Sources keep their own licenses; see each artifact's credits.

## What's here

- [Caves and landscape](artifacts/qumran-map/): Leaflet map of two approximate site locations and a sourced catalogue of Caves 1–11. Cave coordinates remain unverified and are explicitly unmapped.
- [A discovery still unfolding](artifacts/timeline/): 16 interactive milestones from the 1946/47 discovery through radiocarbon and AI research, with a 2026 editorial checkpoint.
- [A Qumran scroll jar](artifacts/jar/): procedural three.js model of KhQ 1474, based on published jar and lid dimensions; includes a 10 cm scale bar.
- [Shrine of the Book](artifacts/shrine-photo/): three Commons photos, with per-image credits and Creative Commons reuse terms.
- [Cite an archaeological source](skills/cite-an-archaeological-source/SKILL.md): guidance for members' agents on provenance, dating uncertainty, coordinates and media rights.

Each artifact keeps readable data and a `SOURCES.md` beside the visual. Web artifacts require internet access for pinned libraries and map tiles; they use relative URLs for their own data and do not require browser storage. Source access was checked on 30 October 2026. The timeline describes selected verified milestones, not an exhaustive claim to cover every study through that date.
