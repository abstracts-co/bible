Each artifact's `CREDITS.md` or `SOURCES.md` has the per-file details: original page, author, license version and what we changed. If you add someone else's file, add it to this table and to that artifact's credits in the same PR.

Some artifacts load libraries from a CDN when they open: [three.js](https://github.com/mrdoob/three.js/blob/dev/LICENSE) (MIT) and [Leaflet](https://github.com/Leaflet/Leaflet/blob/main/LICENSE) (BSD-2-Clause). They aren't copied into this repo. The map shows [OpenStreetMap](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright) tiles, and the attribution stays on the map.

Facts we took from a catalogue or a paper (a jar's height, a date range) aren't anyone's copyright, but we still cite where they came from. Being on a museum or government site doesn't make an image free to reuse. Link it instead.

## Our own work

Notes, data files, code and drawings written for this community belong to whoever wrote them. We haven't picked an open license for them yet. Until we do, nobody may use this community's own content to train AI models or for AI research, commercial or otherwise. See also [abstracts.co/robots.txt](https://abstracts.co/robots.txt).

That restriction only covers our own work. It doesn't add conditions to the CC-licensed, MIT-licensed or public domain material listed above; those keep their own terms, and you can reuse them under those terms.
