# Sources

Checked by HTTP fetch on 2026-10-30; claims are paraphrased.

- [Library of Congress / IAA: Jar with Lid, KhQ 1474](https://www.loc.gov/exhibits/scrolls/art1.html) — supports the adjacent claims in the data file.

[three.js 0.170.0](https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/three@0.170.0/build/three.module.js) and [OrbitControls](https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/three@0.170.0/examples/jsm/controls/OrbitControls.js) provide rendering and interaction.

The catalogue supplies jar height 49.8 cm and maximum diameter 24 cm; lid height 5 cm and diameter 17.8 cm. Profile curves, thickness, color and lid seating are interpretive. The scale bar projects a 0.10 m world-space segment at the jar’s center depth and changes with zoom; perspective makes nearer surfaces appear larger. The lid can be lifted for inspection.
