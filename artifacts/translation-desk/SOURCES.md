# Sources

Both texts were downloaded on 1 November 2026 and copied into `passages.json` by hand, then checked line by line against the downloaded files. Verse numbers, line breaks and indentation follow each edition. Nothing is reworded.

## World English Bible (WEB)

- Edition: World English Bible, "2020 stable text edition", the 66-book protocanon (`engwebp`). The eBible.org page says the HTML was generated on 30 Oct 2026 from source files of the same date.
- File: <https://ebible.org/engwebp/MRK01.htm> (Mark 1), SHA-256 `7389ddd892065dcfe12eae7e86a4e202952908aa6ce5536970ec77415c1e1674`
- License: public domain. From <https://ebible.org/engwebp/copyright.htm>: "The World English Bible is in the Public Domain." "World English Bible" is a trademark of eBible.org, and they ask that changed text not be called the World English Bible. This is unchanged.
- The three footnotes shown (on "Behold", and the Malachi 3:1 and Isaiah 40:3 references) are the WEB's own, copied as they appear.

## Open English Bible (OEB)

- Edition: Open English Bible release 2025.6, US spelling, USFM source.
- File: <https://openenglishbible.org/oeb/2025.6/OEB-2025.6-US.usfm.zip>, SHA-256 `604efec4dc3e35d3a4e45d7dc6667d024819ef701f1b4a45eeb90181b1e795cb`. Mark is `usfm/41-Mark.usfm` in the zip, SHA-256 `e07b6f413317bff41815a96782c3e8744146582d3e8d655f8e0d4c89ed8a6b36`.
- License: CC0, as stated on <https://openenglishbible.org/> ("The OEB is under a Creative Commons Zero licence - that is, it is in the public domain"), with a link to <https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/>.
- The heading "The Preparation" is the OEB's own section heading (`\s`). The file's header notes give its original base text as the Twentieth Century New Testament.

## What this isn't

It's two published translations next to each other. There's no Greek here and no argument about which reading is right. That's a separate contribution, and it needs a language reviewer (see CONTRIBUTING.md).
