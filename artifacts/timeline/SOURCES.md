## Reading the dates

Year and month precision is preserved in `date`; ranges are not converted to invented days. The display spaces milestones as a chronology, not a linear time scale. The last entry is an editorial research-status checkpoint.

The 2017 report is from an excavation participant's institution. The Hebrew University announcement was blocked by a security interstitial. The 2021 government page was blocked; the verified NBC report explicitly attributes the announcement to the IAA. Israel Museum's legacy digital site failed TLS; the project partner's original launch announcement verifies 2011. The Huntington site returned a security checkpoint, so its 1991 access decision is sourced to the Library of Congress.

The 2025 paper's 27 valid radiocarbon dates, 24 training samples and 135 predictions describe different datasets. Enoch's ranges are model outputs, not certain calendar dates. The paper discusses selecting accepted calibrated ranges using palaeographic knowledge; the model is not independent of all palaeographic assumptions.
